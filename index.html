Hushgem

Sealed-bid gem auctions

The idea

Everyone starts with a few coins and a hidden hand of gems. The hand gems never score for you. They only get revealed, and the revealed gems set what the gems you buy are worth.

Each lot

A card is flipped and everyone bids in secret. The highest bid wins and pays it to the bank. Ties go clockwise from the last winner. Then the winner reveals one hand gem, whatever the lot was.

The lots

1 Gem takes the oldest of the two face-up gems, 2 Gems takes both. Invest: your bid is locked until the end, then returned with +5 or +10. Loan: take 10 or 20 coins now, repay at the end. Only loans let you bid more than your cash.

Missions

Four are open. Hold the gems a mission asks for in your collection and it is yours, for coins at the end. Only the first player to complete it gets it.

Scoring

The game ends when the last gem is bought and every hand is revealed. Score = coins + investments (bid back + return) + missions + gems − loans. Each gem you bought is worth the chart value for the number of that color in the display. Bought gems never count toward the display.

Setup

3 players: 35 coins, 5 hand gems. 4 players: 25 coins, 4 hand gems. 5 players: 20 coins, 3 hand gems. Thirty gems, six of each color.

These rules are written in our own words and may differ from the original game in small ways. The bots bid by a simple value-per-coin rule and are a sparring partner, not the strongest players.

Hushgem is an unofficial, free fan-made practice tool based on MegaGem, a game from Jane Street. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jane Street, uses none of the official art or text, and has no ads or payments. If Jane Street asks, it comes down.
